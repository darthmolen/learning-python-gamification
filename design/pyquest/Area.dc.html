The brief

Every variable so far has held exactly one thing. That runs out the moment a player has an inventory or a crafting table has a book of recipes. This area is the four shapes Python gives you for holding many things at once — list, dict, set, tuple — and, more importantly, the rule for choosing between them.

It ends with a working crafting simulator that you can hand to somebody else.
