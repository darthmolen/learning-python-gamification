verify.py and the missing search root
=====================================
Track: learner-setup-repairs        Date: 2026-09-10

THE DEFECT
----------
SEARCH = (ROOT / "practices", ROOT / "reference")

`reference/` is the DM's copy. curriculum/area-0/reference/README.md line 3:
"**This directory is yours, not the learner's.** Do not copy it onto the learner's machine."

pathlib.Path.rglob on a directory that does not exist yields nothing rather than raising, so on
a learner's machine the harness counted fewer files, printed the smaller number as if it were
the whole, and exited 0. Areas 0, 1 and 2 had no guard at all. Area 3 had `if d.exists()` and
skipped silently, which is better and still silent.

curriculum/area-2/practices/README.md already argues against exactly this:
"A harness that quietly counts zero and prints a reassuring result is worse than one that names
what it cannot check."  The harness it describes is this one.

load-bearing rather than decorative.
