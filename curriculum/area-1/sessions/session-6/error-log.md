## Then answer these

**1. Three of the six produced no error message at all. Which three?**


**2. Of those three, one hung, one drew too few lines, and one drew the right number
of lines in the wrong directions. Which is which?**


**3. `b2` never stopped and `b5` never started. Both of those are loops going round
the wrong number of times. What is the wrong number in each case?**


**4. `b5` complained about a name that is spelled correctly and is right there in the
file three lines above. When does the name in a `for` line actually get created?**


**5. `b4`'s number was 8 and it was still the wrong kind of 8. Which of Area 0's four
kinds was it, and which one did `range` want?**


**6. You could prove `b6` was broken with arithmetic, without looking at the picture
at all. Show the sum.**


**7. Of the six, which would have been hardest to find if nobody had told you it was
broken? Say why. Then say which was easiest, and why the easy one was easy.**
