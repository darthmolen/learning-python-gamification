# Out Of Line

Broken sigil 4, from session 3. In Python, blank space at the start of a line is not
decoration. It means something, and one of these lines starts in the wrong place.

## What to do

1. **Run it.** Write down the error's name and its line number.
2. **Compare it with Never Closed.** These two errors are cousins — one of them has the other
   one's name inside it. Say how you can tell them apart.
3. **Put the line back where it belongs.**

## The rule that matters

**Move the line. Do not delete it.**

All three orders stay: two sides and the corner between them. An indented line that is gone
is not an indented line that is fixed.

## Done when

It runs without complaint and draws two hundred-step sides with a right-angle turn between
them.
